A letter

14 October 2026

Dear Ica,

I know you've got the tes tertulis and the wawancara coming up, and I know how much studying you've been doing for it. I wanted to write this down so you can come back to it whenever you need to.

I'm not going to pretend I can make it less stressful. I can't, and I'd feel silly saying otherwise. But I want you to know I'm in your corner for both. The test and the interview.

For the test, trust what you've studied. You've put in the hours, and it's in there even on the days your head feels full. For the interview, just be yourself. That's already more than enough.

I also know the nerves can get loud. If you're nervous, tell me. If you need to vent after a long study day, I'll listen, and I won't try to fix anything unless you ask. And if you need a break from all of it, we can talk about something else entirely. Anything at all.

Please take care of yourself too. Eat properly, drink water, and sleep when you can. I know it feels like every hour counts right now, but you'll do better with a rested head than with one more hour of pushing through.

Some days will feel good and some days will feel awful. Both are normal. You don't have to be perfect on any of them.

I'm proud of you for going for this. Really. Whatever happens at the end of it, I'm on your side, and that doesn't depend on the results.

Keefa
